A survival, horror and stealth overhaul that gives hostile mobs real senses:
mobs hear footsteps, blocks, gunshots (with suppressors!) and Simple Voice Chat through walls, spot torchlight vs darkness, and track bleeding trails — with a client Stealth HUD and sleek 2-tab GUI.

Server-side AI works on its own — vanilla clients join with nothing installed. Paper, Purpur, Folia or Spigot server? Use the Mob Senses Bukkit plugin.

 

✦ Hearing & Wall Occlusion — sprinting is loud, sneaking is quiet, wool and carpet muffle footsteps while gravel and metal give you away. Breaking and placing blocks, doors, chests, combat, arrows, explosions, bells, note blocks and eating make noise too. Alerted mobs walk to where the sound came from, search the spot and return to their business. Stone walls (×0.64), wool (×0.53), rain, thunderstorms, Feather Falling boots and Swift Sneak leggings muffle noise, wood and glass block less, and open doors or trapdoors let sound pass.

✦ Firearms, Suppressors & Bullet Lures — native support for TaCZ (Timeless and Classics Zero), Vic's Point Blank, MrCrayfish's Gun Mod, Scorched Guns 2, Superb Warfare and Just Enough Guns. Unsuppressed shots carry across the area (44 blocks) and draw nearby mobs; attaching a suppressor or silencer cuts the shot to close range (16 blocks). Firing a silenced round into a distant wall makes noise where the bullet lands (20 blocks) so you can distract or lure hordes away. Every weapon can also be tuned individually.

✦ Proximity Voice Chat — with Simple Voice Chat installed, mobs hear you speak. Shouting carries far while whispering stays close; loudness is calibrated automatically against each player's own normal speaking voice, so a quiet microphone gives no free stealth. Works cleanly alongside Sound Physics Remastered.

✦ Torchlight & Darkness Stealth — holding a torch, lantern or glowstone exposes you from 50% farther away; sneaking in the dark or drinking an Invisibility potion (without held light) hides you and lets distant pursuers lose track of you.

✦ Blood Scent & Horde Alerts — badly wounded players (at or below 50% health) leave an in-memory scent trail with crimson blood droplets on the ground that zombies and spiders track. Alerted mobs can optionally call nearby allies to investigate as a horde — with zero dummy marker entities spawned in the world.

 

✦ Tactical Stealth HUD — choose between the chamfered Tactical card (stealth eye, 30%/65% threshold ticks, transient peak-hold marker) and the slim Minimal bar strip. Shows live noise, estimated audible radius (~Xm), light exposure, blood scent and [Voice] / [Whisper] badges, with smooth auto-hide when quiet.

✦ Sleek 2-Tab Settings GUI & Keybind — dark-slate in-game settings screen (Stealth HUD with live interactive preview + Senses & World), opened from Mod Menu (Fabric/Quilt), Mods → Config (Forge/NeoForge), your own keybind (Mob Senses…), or /mshud on any server without operator permissions. Translated into 7 languages.

✦ Lightweight Client ↔ Server Sync — when joining a modded or Paper/Folia server running Mob Senses, the HUD automatically syncs the active server or dimension preset, multipliers and fair-play HUD rules so your ~Xm distance readout stays accurate.

 

Players without the mod get the full mob AI experience — vanilla clients need nothing installed.

✦ Presets & Per-World Balance — switch live between Off (vanilla), Subtle (builders), Default (survival) and Hardcore (every step counts), or assign separate presets per dimension with /mobsenses world.

✦ Fair Play & Live Operator GUI — hide the client Stealth HUD on competitive PvP or horror servers (allow_client_hud=false). Operators with the client mod tune presets and sliders live right in the Senses & World tab — even on Paper, Purpur and Folia servers.

✦ Clickable Commands, Staff Bypass & Debug Traces — clickable /mobsenses chat controls, /mobsenses debug particle traces showing sound, light and blood paths to alerted mobs, and mobsenses.bypass so vanished staff trigger no extra mob senses.
