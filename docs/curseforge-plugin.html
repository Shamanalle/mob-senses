A survival, horror and stealth plugin for Paper, Purpur, Folia and Spigot (Minecraft 1.16.5 – 26.3):
hostile mobs hear footsteps, blocks, combat and Simple Voice Chat through walls, spot torchlight vs darkness, and track bleeding trails — with zero client mods needed.

Players who also install the optional Mob Senses mod get the Tactical Stealth HUD and sleek 2-tab settings screen synced live with your server.

 

✦ Hearing & Wall Occlusion — sprinting is loud, sneaking is quiet, wool and carpet muffle footsteps while gravel and metal give players away. Breaking and placing blocks, doors, chests, combat, arrows, explosions, bells, note blocks and eating make noise too. Alerted mobs walk to where the sound came from, search the area and return to normal. Stone walls (×0.64), wool (×0.53), rain, thunderstorms, Feather Falling boots and Swift Sneak leggings muffle noise, while open doors let sound through.

✦ Simple Voice Chat (optional) — with Simple Voice Chat installed, mobs hear players talk. Shouts carry far, whispers only next to them; loudness is calibrated automatically against each player's usual speaking voice, so a quiet microphone gives no unfair stealth.

✦ Torchlight & Darkness Stealth — holding a torch, lantern or glowstone makes players visible from 50% farther away; sneaking in darkness or drinking an Invisibility potion without held light hides players and drops distant pursuit.

✦ Blood Scent & Horde Alerts — badly wounded players (at or below 50% health) leave a fast in-memory scent trail with crimson blood droplets on the ground that zombies and spiders follow, with zero dummy marker entities spawned (safe for Folia region threads and high-player TPS).

 

✦ Presets, Per-World Balance & 45+ Settings — switch between Off, Subtle, Default and Hardcore, assign per-world presets with /mobsenses world, or tune any sound category, wall muffling, darkness stealth, blood trail or listener mob list live with /mobsenses set.

✦ Lightweight Client Sync & Fair Play — automatically sync active server and world presets to players running the client mod so their ~Xm HUD distance stays accurate, or hide their Stealth HUD for competitive PvP and horror events (allow_client_hud=false).

✦ Clickable Commands, Staff Bypass & Debug Traces — clickable buttons in /mobsenses chat replies, /mobsenses debug particle traces for operators, mobsenses.bypass so vanished moderators trigger no extra mob senses, and messages localized in 7 languages.

Operators who also install the client mod can tune the plugin's presets and sliders live in its Senses & World tab.

 

1. Put mob-senses-bukkit-*.jar into plugins/. Simple Voice Chat is optional and detected automatically.

2. Start the server. Settings live in plugins/MobSenses/mobsenses-server.properties (reloaded live on save) and can all be changed in game with /mobsenses.

Supports Paper, Purpur, Folia (folia-supported: true), Spigot and Bukkit across Minecraft 1.16.5 – 26.3 in a single universal jar (Java 8+ on 1.16.5, Java 17+ on 1.18+). Running Fabric, Quilt, Forge or NeoForge instead? Install the mod on the server instead.
